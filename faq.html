FAQ

Questions, answered.

The things people ask us most. If yours isn't here, get in touch and we'll happily help.

How much does a website cost?

Websites start from NZ$2,900 plus GST. We have three packages: Launch from NZ$2,900, up to 5 pages for startups, sole traders and small businesses; Growth from NZ$5,900, up to 10 pages with a bespoke, conversion-focused design, local search foundations and enquiry tracking, which is the one we recommend for most businesses; and Scale from NZ$9,900, up to 20 pages with advanced functionality and integrations. Online shops, member areas, custom applications and complex bookings are quoted separately. We confirm every project in a written quote, so you know the full cost before we begin.

How does payment work?

You pay for your website upfront, in one of two ways: 50% before we start and 50% before your website goes live, or in three stages: 40% to start, 30% when you approve the design and 30% before launch. All prices are in NZ dollars and exclude GST.

Do I have to pay a monthly fee?

No. Monthly care plans are optional and separate from your website build. They keep your site hosted, secure, backed up and up to date: Essential is NZ$99 a month, Growth Care NZ$179 a month and Premium Care NZ$299 a month, plus GST. Each includes a set amount of update time each month, from 15 minutes to 2 hours, and unused time does not roll over. Bigger changes and new features are quoted separately.

Can you help us get found and win more enquiries after launch?

Yes. Alongside care plans we offer ongoing SEO and digital marketing partnerships, from NZ$450 a month plus GST for SEO and local SEO, with clear deliverables agreed up front. Advertising budgets are paid separately.

Where are you based, and do you work outside Christchurch?

We are based in Ōtautahi Christchurch and work remotely, so we work with businesses across New Zealand.

What are your opening hours?

We work remotely from Christchurch, by appointment only. Call or email and we will find a time that suits you.

How do I get started?

Call us on +64 22 368 0811, email enquiries@tovadigital.co.nz, or use the form on our contact page. We reply within one working day.

What services do you offer?

We offer three crafts: Experience & Design (websites and brands), Technology & Development (the build behind the site), and Marketing & Growth (search, content and digital marketing).

Who will I work with?

Tova Digital is a small studio where design and engineering sit under one roof. You deal with us directly, from the first conversation through to launch and beyond.

Tell us about your business and we'll reply within one working day.
