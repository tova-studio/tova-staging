About us

Good things, made with care.

Tova Digital is a small Christchurch studio with two people at its heart — one who designs, one who engineers — and a shared belief that the way you do the work matters as much as the work itself.

( 01 ) Our story

We started Tova because too many good businesses were being let down by websites that looked nice but didn't work — or worked but didn't feel like them.

Between us we bring a background in UX, web design and branding, alongside programming, network engineering and the kind of problem-solving that keeps a site fast, secure and reliable.

We work from Ōtautahi Christchurch, close to the bays and harbours of Banks Peninsula that shape so much of how we see things: calm, open, full of light.

( 02 ) Our name

Tova means good.

We chose it because it's simple, and because it's what we hope people say about working with us. We take our cue from light and water: light makes things clear, water brings things to life. That's what we want our work to do for yours.

Our faith is the foundation of how we run Tova — with honesty, generosity and care for every person we work with.

( 03 ) The people

Two makers, one standard.

Co-founder

Lily Barnett

Design, UX, brand & marketing

Lily leads design, user experience, brand and digital marketing at Tova, shaping how every website looks, feels and works for the people who use it, and how it gets found. A web designer and digital marketer, Lily received the number one digital marketing award across Ray White Australasia, has led website design projects from concept through to launch for Team Mullins and Genesis Projects, runs social media and web for First National Progressive Real Estate, and works as a Digital Marketing Specialist at Digital Influence.

Co-founder

Hushdon Tohovaka

Engineering, networks & strategy

Hushdon works across software engineering, networks and quality testing. Hushdon has worked at Allied Telesis, a network technology company, as a Software Engineer, Network Test Engineer and GUI Automation Test Engineer, building an auto-inventory system for network testing and automating tests for network management software. At Tova, Hushdon builds the technology behind each website and keeps it fast, secure and reliable.
